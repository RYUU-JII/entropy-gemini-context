# Plume product owners

`plume` is the shipping Zen bonsai model. This directory contains its promoted
product implementation. `g15_4`, `classic2` and `classic` are explicit comparison
models; the detached research pages under `scripts/dev/` are not product owners.

The active contract is
[Zen Bonsai Product Engine](../../../../../../../docs/architecture/themes/zen/zen-g15-product-engine.md).
Read the Plume sections first: the historical G15.4 sections govern its comparison
path. Shared hooks and configuration under `runtime/classic/` also run Plume;
those filenames do not select the model. `model/ZenBonsaiModelCatalog.js` does.

Public structural disclosure consumes progression's canonical `bonsaiRelease`:
five non-consecutive qualified-use days reveal the first full form, with one
credit at most per successfully recorded managed protected-visit activity day.
Plume consumes that structural growth separately from current bloom/activation.
Natural weather is independent of habit evidence and affects world lighting,
rain and petals through the canonical environment owner. Seven-day calibration
and confidence windows are separate from disclosure; no 30-day calendar-driven
post-maturity growth or seasonal cycle is implemented. The normalized bud-to-bloom projection
does not create an extra qualified day or an elapsed-time opening animation.
Canonical day 4 projects growth `0.948` and pre-bloom anticipation; day 5 is the
exact full-growth endpoint. Current bloom and activation still determine the
visible flowers and petal supply on that same structure.

The release ratio is linear; structural growth uses smoothstep and reaches
`0.5, 0.552, 0.676, 0.824, 0.948, 1` across days 0–5. Its recorded three-year
spring/summer/winter timeline drives shoots, flower-bud induction, dormancy and
wood radius; it is not a calendar-season feature. Wood development and the fixed
camera/reading envelopes consume that history. Preparation cleanup preserves
the growth curve and complete organ histories. Full audit observations remain
in generation/public study; the private Worker transport is the compact product
contract described below.

## Product transport and updates

observations' complete organ rows and attachments, the complete F4 and camera
histories, tray height/origin and durable identity. Axes, events, ledgers, metrics,
Cloud receipts and policy remain in generation/public study. The strict Worker
result validator rejects full/unknown-schema studies; a named adapter supplies
the common live projection body. Public full-study/error contracts remain exact.
Compact product snapshot metadata excludes only the same audit fields; organ,
development, camera, reading-space and render output stay equal. This reduces
retention and transport. Full generation and validation still occur; the closed
generated preparation is compacted before the last deep freeze, which visits only
retained product descendants. Public/generated full-study entries keep their
complete freeze and arbitrary-input behavior.

Only generated product preparation selects the Cloud staging owner and receipt-free
mature spring camera geometry. They preserve the full nine-observation anatomy and
validators while avoiding audit/receipt copies that never enter product transport.
The product finalizer records a weak camera identity proof only after complete
recursive freeze. GeneratedRequest carries it into the initial prepared projector,
which skips that camera subtree on its second freeze. Shallow frozen wrappers and
native clones cannot create this proof; public/full generated projections still
visit every descendant. Proof membership keeps no graph alive.

Subsequent PROJECT uses `zen_bonsai_plume_projection_study_v1` without camera
history and returns `zen_bonsai_plume_projected_snapshot_v1`. Both schemas reject
camera fields. The mounted preparation binding carries exact source/generation
identity; the latest active sequence alone may assemble a full topology with
that binding's retained camera array. Complete native-cloned immutable admission
has one owner in LifeAppearance; weak proofs keep no graph alive. Camera identity
is reused across projections, preserving the existing reading-envelope memo.
PREPARE validates identity/target/life immediately and keeps its existing render-plan
freeze. Complete sharing proof is lazy until the first same-growth rebind; structural
PROJECT assembly obtains it once. Boot gains no duplicate full graph traversal.

Equal normalized growth rebinds the current admitted pose to latest paint controls
and complete life/phenotype using small immutable wrappers. No Worker request or
structural transform occurs. Growth changes use PROJECT. Phase/wind/confidence
and other life metadata still reach petal/biotic consumers. Full request keys,
pending state, canonical render validation and a new frame ACK remain authoritative.

Mounted surfaces coalesce active structural bursts: one PROJECT runs, intermediate
waiting inputs are skipped, and only the latest demand can accept its result.
Parking/unmount cancel physical and waiting work immediately. Cultivation

The live Pixi renderer retains bounded atlas/crop/sprite resources on resize,

## Material and resolution pitfalls

Petal supply reads `topology.lifeState` from the same accepted immutable Plume
picture as its flower/development receipt. A pending canonical lifecycle refresh
can withhold the next input while `useZenBonsaiPlumeTopology` retains that picture;
Home and Analysis must not mix the retained flowers with the next requested life.
Missing life on an accepted topology remains an explicit contract error.

`ZenBonsaiPlumePresentation` uses `zenBonsaiInkTint` for the support palette.
Landscape `frame-seam` alone uses canonical shell L3 top/bottom for its exposed
frame planes and muted border for the recess, joining the pressure rail without
applying a second tree surface tint. `zenHomeFrameSeamRenderer` caches the resolved
palette by immutable environment and surface mapper; motion frames reuse it.
The existing world-lit soil, moss strokes and contact shadow retain their depth.
Granite black washes and mineral flecks no longer repaint the architectural top
or front. The mesh and bevel stay fixed. Planter materials keep their projection.
Generic wood/accent tint functions do not recolor Plume branches or flowers.
Change the actual material owners above, not a comparison renderer. Preserve
the intentional bloom/activation opacity response.

The F4-inspired wood bands belong to the existing opaque tube fill. Their tone
and positions are shared along each axis; do not reintroduce a short light dash
per internode, or import the detached F4 ribbon/junction geometry. The shared
gradient gains stops, not additional strokes or atlas layers. This preserves
the product's connected surface and Canvas/Pixi command ordering.

Atlas v4 bakes real projected petal cups across elevation, opening and azimuth,
then reuses those pixels in three alpha-preserving depth-tone banks.
The shared sakura lamina adapts F4's tapered shoulders and shallow cleft instead
of round heart lobes. A broad diffuse light body and shaded fold restore value
separation; the small throat remains warm. The same body gradient and outline
serve airborne/landed petals. No research renderer is imported into product.
The five petals have modest fixed rise/twist/length variation and
diffuse normal-based shade baked into those same 72 poses. Shared sakura pigment
and lamina also keep airborne/landed petals consistent. Do not add dynamic
per-petal sprites or extra depth banks to express this material detail.
An instance uses a uniform screen rotation/reflection, not an oblique squeeze of
the top view. Do not restore cosine compensation or the command-plan foldWidth
matrix squeeze: they would flatten the baked side silhouette again.

Crown appearance v5 releases a triple's redundant interior only when two
protected neighbors from other clusters are closer than the sum of their raw
rest radii. The previous 0.75-radius-sum clearance left many occupied pockets.

## Validation and comparisons

The current Home first-bloom presentation accepts an optional transient
`bloomPresentationProgress` as null or an ordinary array of exactly five unit
numbers, outside the signed plan and cache signatures. The tiny
`ZenBonsaiPlumeBloomPresentationContract.js` owns region count, masks and the
900 ms region duration. Scalar progress is rejected.
`ZenBonsaiPlumeBloomRegions.js` partitions actual projected flower centers once
per immutable display plan. Positive-alpha flowers seed five nonempty spatial
groups; fewer than five visible flowers or distinct centers returns explicit
ineligibility. Flower IDs sort lexicographically before farthest-point seeding;
up to eight bounded centroid/medoid refinements retain an actual flower as each
representative. Distance ties use flower ID, and final region IDs sort medoids
by screen x, then y and ID. Membership is independent of DTO order and uniform
camera scale/translation. Invisible organs receive a region but never host a
target. Region points/bounds are canopy-local device pixels; Home adds its
existing canopy viewport offset before converting through the actual canvas
rect. These bounds contain flower centers, not an alpha-mask silhouette.
`ZenBonsaiPlumeBloomPresentation.js` compiles deterministic spatial/organ-ID
delays and region membership once per immutable display plan. Each flower reads
only its region's progress. The existing shared flower atlas already
contains closed, middle and open poses; adjacent poses crossfade with continuous
radius compensation in both Pixi and Canvas. Pending progress zero needs only
the original flower sprites. During actual crossfades, Pixi lazily adds at most
one companion per flower, reusing the existing atlas. A completed region restores
its exact original tile, matrix and alpha and releases its companions; null
restores the whole original endpoint.
Optional `bloomInvitation` carries only active admission, the activated-region
mask and existing-loop elapsed milliseconds. Only each unactivated region's
actual representative bud breathes twice: 1200 ms cycles staggered 85 ms by
region, at most four percent scale and 0.08 additional alpha, bounded by one.
After the two cycles it is static. Both backends share this pose function; the
invitation adds no sprite, filter, texture upload or painter pass.
No anatomy, wood atlas, flower atlas or render plan is regenerated by progress.
This expresses unfolding at the final blossom positions; it does not morph the
previous day's wood-baked tip buds one-to-one into the next day's flower organs.

`scripts/ci/check-zen-bonsai-architecture.mjs` guards product/research separation,
model roles, signed-plan ownership and the shared Home canopy seam. The worker
artifact guards additionally keep generation code out of the renderer closure.
