# Zen Pond Boot Spec

- Path policy: planning-allowed
- Canonical for: Zen pond boot loading direction

Last Updated: 2026-04-18

## Goal

Replace the temporary `zen` boot screen with a pond-first loading scene that turns waiting into a calm pause.

The screen should feel like:

- stillness arriving before interaction
- a quiet garden taking one breath
- a warm, low-stress transition into the theme

## Visual Direction

- Base the scene on warm paper, soft stone, pale mist, and a shallow pond surface.
- Keep the composition centered and sparse. One pond is the focus.
- Avoid "system boot" language such as scan lines, grid tracks, or aggressive glyph framing.
- Preserve the premium, high-fidelity tone of the `zen` theme.

## Composition

- Full-screen paper backdrop with subtle grain.
- One shallow elliptical pond slightly below center.
- A soft shadow under the pond so it feels set into the surface.
- Minimal text above and below the pond with generous spacing.
- No progress bar, spinner, or bouncing dots.

## Motion

- The pond breathes with a very slow 6-8 second loop.
- Two or three ripple rings expand softly from the center with long fade tails.
- A faint cloud-shadow or reflected light drift crosses the pond slowly.
- Motion must stay readable but never demand attention.

## Copy

- Eyebrow: quiet, poetic, and light.
- Title: keep the theme title.
- Body/status: describe settling, stillness, or light arriving.
- Copy should support waiting, not narrate technical boot steps.

## Transition

- The boot scene should feel like it is already part of the world.
- When loading completes, the screen should disappear cleanly without a hard flash.
- The pond scene must not visually fight the first real dashboard frame.

## Guardrails

- Do not introduce alarm-like motion, high-contrast flashes, or noisy flicker.
- Do not overload the scene with petals, koi, or multiple ornaments.
- Do not make the screen feel empty by removing all motion; the calm should still feel alive.
